# Sound effects license

Punch / impact samples in this directory are derived from **Kenney — Impact
Sounds** (https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), licensed
**Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)** — public domain, free for personal,
educational and commercial use, attribution not required.

Original: created/distributed by Kenney, 19-12-2019. License text:
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Modifications: trimmed to short impact windows, volume-normalized and fade-out
applied with ffmpeg to suit fast fighting-game hits. Filenames:
`punch_{light,medium,heavy}_*`, `block_0`, `parry_0`.

These are NOT from Street Fighter / The King of Fighters / SNK or any commercial
ROM — the project's copyright red line (README.md) is respected.
